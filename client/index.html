Tally vs GST Comparison

Compare book sales with imported GSTR-1 returns, purchases with GSTR-2B, and totals with filed GSTR-3B.

Import a Tally Transactions.json export. Sales, purchases, credit notes, and debit notes will be split into the saved Tally comparison registers.

Imported Tally registers are kept separate from cashbook records and replace the previous import of the same type.
